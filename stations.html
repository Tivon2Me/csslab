Supported Plug Standards

  1. CCS (Combined Charging System): Standard for European and American EVs.
  2. NACS (North American Charging Standard): Native standard for Tesla and supported EV models.
  3. Type 2 (Mennekes): Universal standard for AC Level 2 charging.
  4. CHAdeMO: Supported fast-charging connector for Japanese EV models.
